# Decisions

* [A mint starts from the last run](a-mint-starts-from-the-last-run.md) - The checkpoints at the last run are the value at that W, so only the buckets the events since then touched can differ; the full mint remains as oracle and repair.
* [A tombstone is an event, not an artifact version](tombstone-is-an-event-not-an-artifact.md) - Retraction appends a tombstoned anam-event against the current version and writes no artifact, because an Anam artifact has nowhere to carry a status.
* [Artifact writes are atomic, temp file and rename](artifact-writes-are-atomic.md) - A checkpoint value, served value or receipt is written to a nonce-named temp file with O_EXCL, synced, and renamed under its hash; equal bytes are idempotent and different bytes at one address are corruption.
* [Bucket on fact-time, monthly by default, subdivided by a byte budget](bucket-on-fact-time-subdivide-by-budget.md) - Checkpoints partition the timeline by calendar window over fact-time; a bucket over checkpoint_budget splits a year into months and a month into days, so bytes decide when and the calendar decides where.
* [Commands that only read open the ledger read-only](readers-open-the-ledger-read-only.md) - Every read command and every daemon read route uses a mode=ro handle, so SQLite itself refuses a write; only init, ingest, retract --yes and the daemon open it read-write.
* [Definitions live in the operational store, not the ledger](definitions-live-in-the-operational-store.md) - A Definition is how Anam interprets, not what it knew; every version is held in tracking.db, provisioned from the binary or from the importer that registers it, and a registered version is immutable.
* [Fact-time is promoted out of the payload at the gate](fact-time-is-promoted-at-the-gate.md) - The Definition's time_semantics pointer, Go layout and IANA zone turn a payload field into a first-class UTC column, so the timeline orders and filters without opening a payload.
* [Health measures use Open mHealth bodies where one exists](open-mhealth-is-the-payload-vocabulary.md) - health.blood-pressure v4 and later, and exercise.session, conform to a pinned Open mHealth (IEEE 1752) schema, with the source's own text kept beside it.
* [Importers live outside the binary, as ductile plugins](importers-live-outside-the-binary.md) - A source is a Python plugin in anam-importers that knows one vendor and shapes rows to a Definition; one generic anam_submit posts under a per-source token; Anam keeps the Definitions, the gate and the record and knows no vendor.
* [Mint cadence follows the ledger](mint-cadence-follows-the-ledger.md) - The daemon mints after every write that moved W, as its own write on the queue: at once after a declared ingest, and after 2 s of quiet following importer batches.
* [One write worker in the daemon](one-write-queue-in-the-daemon.md) - Every ledger and operational-store write inside the daemon is a job on one channel run by one goroutine under the job's deadline; reads use read-only handles and take no turn.
* [Per-source importer tokens are the authenticated pillar](importer-tokens-are-the-authenticated-pillar.md) - Each importer has its own token; the daemon holds only its SHA-256 beside its source and types; the token names the source, opens three routes, and cannot read the record.
* [Publication is not an anam-event](publication-is-not-an-anam-event.md) - Serving changes nothing Anam knows, so there is no published event; a receipt records what was shown, and a facet's published time is the earliest served_at over the receipts naming it.
* [Reads over the daemon are bounded by bytes](reads-over-the-daemon-are-bounded.md) - A timeline, publish or receipt show over max_read_bytes is refused with 413 and the parameters that would narrow it, measured on the wire encoding; a local read and a verify are never bounded.
* [Retraction by complete resend](retraction-by-complete-resend.md) - An importer never tombstones; it labels a resend-everything run as the parts of one snapshot, Anam reports what is live but not carried, and the operator retracts with guards.
* [The gate composes identity from the Definition](the-gate-composes-identity.md) - An envelope may leave source_ref out; the gate composes it from identity_key through one length-prefixed function and refuses one that disagrees, so importers shape payloads and nothing else.
* [The importer's client stays on its origin and trusts only a whole receipt](the-client-trusts-only-a-whole-receipt.md) - anam_submit reports a batch admitted only when the 200 body is a complete admission receipt; no redirect is followed, one deadline bounds the whole call, answers are capped at 8 MiB, and a cut answer is retryable.
* [The ledger is its own file, apart from the operational store](the-ledger-is-its-own-file.md) - Two SQLite files, no transaction across them; the derived tier catches up by replay and is behind after a crash, never wrong.
* [The person is the substrate](the-inversion-person-as-substrate.md) - Anam inverts Arch's keystone: the person, not the episode, is the unit of storage, and the whole-life read is the default; minting stays per bucket.
* [The substrate is accidental: only internal/store names SQLite](the-substrate-is-accidental.md) - The problem is a personal record, not a store; scripts/check.sh fails the build if database/sql or sqlite is named outside internal/store, or a capture worker imports the store.
* [Two clocks, not four](two-clocks-not-four.md) - A facet carries fact-time and nothing else; an anam-event carries anam-time and nothing else; recorded-time and ingested-time were deleted as duplicates.
