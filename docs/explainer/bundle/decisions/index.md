# Decisions

* [A distinction goes in the cheapest place that lets its reader ask cleanly](where-a-distinction-is-encoded.md) - A type is domain.concept[.specialisation…]; a distinction lives in the type name, the Definition's meta, a payload field, provenance, an edge or a projection, and is promoted up that list only when something forces it.
* [A hard-coded value is a default or an invariant](a-hard-coded-value-is-a-default-or-an-invariant.md) - A value someone could reasonably choose differently is a config key with a shipped default and a range check; a value whose change breaks correctness or a contract stays a constant with a comment saying why; no test hook changes either.
* [A mint starts from the last run](a-mint-starts-from-the-last-run.md) - The checkpoints at the last run are the value at that W, so only the buckets the events since then touched can differ; the full mint remains as oracle and repair.
* [A read may select by payload; the store never does](a-read-may-select-by-payload-the-store-never-does.md) - Payload opacity is a storage rule, not a ceiling on what a person may ask of their own record; a query selects and joins by any schema field, read from the served copy through a projection, and the store still never decomposes or indexes a payload.
* [A person's words are a note, and a model's reading is a draft](a-persons-words-are-a-note-and-a-models-reading-is-a-draft.md) - A conversation is an importer; the person's words are saved at once and verbatim as a record.edge under source conversation, which may name no facet yet; a model's reading is a second source whose facts are drafts the person endorses by a new version.
* [A token names a principal with a role](tokens-carry-roles.md) - Every caller is a principal (name, role, token hash); importers keep their source and types, and readers and operators get no source; a route names the roles it admits and the handlers never branch on who is calling.
* [A tombstone is an event, not an artifact version](tombstone-is-an-event-not-an-artifact.md) - Retraction appends a tombstoned anam-event against the current version and writes no artifact, because an Anam artifact has nowhere to carry a status.
* [Bucket on fact-time, monthly by default, subdivided by a byte budget](bucket-on-fact-time-subdivide-by-budget.md) - Checkpoints partition the timeline by calendar window over fact-time; a bucket over checkpoint_budget splits a year into months and a month into days, so bytes decide when and the calendar decides where.
* [Commands that only read open the ledger read-only](readers-open-the-ledger-read-only.md) - Every read command and every daemon read route uses a mode=ro handle, so SQLite itself refuses a write; only init, retract --yes and the daemon open it read-write.
* [Definitions live in the operational store, not the ledger](definitions-live-in-the-operational-store.md) - A Definition is how Anam interprets, not what it knew; every version is held in tracking.db, provisioned from the binary or from the importer that registers it, and a registered version is immutable.
* [Every who-may rule is one static policy package](who-may-is-one-static-policy-package.md) - internal/policy answers every who-may question as pure Go over the request and the importers file's principals, never a payload; the handlers branch on nothing else about the caller; the flip to a Datalog policy base waits for a stated trigger.
* [Fact-time is promoted out of the payload at the gate](fact-time-is-promoted-at-the-gate.md) - The Definition's time_semantics pointer, Go layout and IANA zone turn a payload field into a first-class UTC column, so the timeline orders and filters without opening a payload.
* [Health measures use Open mHealth bodies where one exists](open-mhealth-is-the-payload-vocabulary.md) - health.blood-pressure v4 and later, and the leaves of activity.exercise-session, conform to a pinned Open mHealth (IEEE 1752) schema, with the source's own text kept beside it.
* [Importers live outside the binary, as ductile plugins](importers-live-outside-the-binary.md) - A source is a Python plugin in anam-importers that knows one vendor and shapes rows to a Definition; one generic anam_submit posts under a per-source token; Anam keeps the Definitions, the gate and the record and knows no vendor.
* [Mint cadence follows the ledger](mint-cadence-follows-the-ledger.md) - The daemon mints after importer batches that moved W, once `mint_quiet` (2 s) passes with no batch, as its own write on the queue; at start it mints if the last run is behind the head.
* [One write worker in the daemon](one-write-queue-in-the-daemon.md) - Every ledger and operational-store write inside the daemon is a job on one channel run by one goroutine under the job's deadline; reads use read-only handles and take no turn.
* [Per-source importer tokens are the authenticated pillar](importer-tokens-are-the-authenticated-pillar.md) - Each importer has its own token; the daemon holds only its SHA-256 beside its source and types; the token names the source, opens the write routes, and an importer reads as a reader does.
* [Publication is not an anam-event](publication-is-not-an-anam-event.md) - Serving changes nothing Anam knows, so there is no published event; a receipt records what was shown, and a facet's published time is the earliest served_at over the receipts naming it.
* [Published reads page by a checked cursor](published-reads-page-by-a-checked-cursor.md) - A published read over max_read_bytes is filed once and answered a page at a time, each page cut from the served value; the cursor names its format, the receipt, the offset and the facet there, and is checked against the receipt, not signed.
* [Reads over the daemon are bounded by bytes](reads-over-the-daemon-are-bounded.md) - A timeline over max_read_bytes is refused with 413 and the parameters that would narrow it, measured on the wire encoding; a published read pages instead (card 92); a local read and a verify are never bounded.
* [Retraction by complete resend](retraction-by-complete-resend.md) - An importer never tombstones; it labels a resend-everything run as the parts of one snapshot, Anam reports what is live but not carried, and the operator retracts with guards.
* [Stored content is a row keyed by the SHA-256 of its bytes](content-is-a-row-keyed-by-its-hash.md) - A checkpoint value, served value, receipt or saved selection is a row of the operational store keyed by the hash of its own bytes; a put of equal bytes is idempotent, different bytes at one key are corruption, and every read re-hashes.
* [The gate composes identity from the Definition](the-gate-composes-identity.md) - An envelope may leave source_ref out; the gate composes it from identity_key through one length-prefixed function and refuses one that disagrees, so importers shape payloads and nothing else.
* [The importer's client stays on its origin and trusts only a whole receipt](the-client-trusts-only-a-whole-receipt.md) - anam_submit reports a batch admitted only when the 200 body is a complete admission receipt; no redirect is followed, one deadline bounds the whole call, answers are capped at 8 MiB, and a cut answer is retryable.
* [The ledger is its own file, apart from the operational store](the-ledger-is-its-own-file.md) - Two SQLite files, no transaction across them; the derived tier catches up by replay and is behind after a crash, never wrong.
* [The person is the substrate](the-inversion-person-as-substrate.md) - The person, not the episode, is the unit of storage, and the whole-life read is the default; minting stays per bucket.
* [The substrate is accidental: only internal/store names SQLite](the-substrate-is-accidental.md) - The problem is a personal record, not a store; scripts/check.sh fails the build if database/sql or sqlite is named outside internal/store, with no exception.
* [Two clocks, not four](two-clocks-not-four.md) - A facet carries fact-time and nothing else; an anam-event carries anam-time and nothing else; recorded-time and ingested-time were deleted as duplicates.
