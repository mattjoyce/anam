A life has no edges, and "longitudinal is system-of-record gravity". Anam makes the person the substrate and accepts that gravity at the scale of one life. The whole-person timeline read is the default and normal mode; a span is an optional lens.

What this does not license: the read default is unbounded, the mint unit is not. Minting stays per [bucket span](/terms/bucket-span.md).
