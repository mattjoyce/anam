A subject's history has no edges (a life has none, and neither does a machine's service), and "longitudinal is system-of-record gravity". Anam makes the subject, what the record is about, the substrate and accepts that gravity at the scale of one subject. The whole-record timeline read is the default and normal mode; a span is an optional lens.

What this does not license: the read default is unbounded, the mint unit is not. Minting stays per [bucket span](/terms/bucket-span.md).
