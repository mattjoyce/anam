type: term
title: The inversion
description: "The one decision that distinguishes Anam from Arch: the person is the substrate and the whole-life read is the default."
timestamp: 2026-10-11T00:00:00Z
---

Arch, the parent architecture, demotes the person to a thin identity index because a life has no edges and "longitudinal is system-of-record gravity". Anam re-promotes the person to substrate and accepts that gravity at the scale of one life. The whole-person timeline read is the default and normal mode; a span is an optional lens.

What the inversion does not license: the read default is unbounded, the mint unit is not. Minting stays per [bucket span](/terms/bucket-span.md).
