type: term
title: Status
description: A facet's current state, derived by the fold from anam-events and never stored; distinct from its immutable birth status.
timestamp: 2026-10-11T00:00:00Z
---

Status is one of admitted, superseded, tombstoned, endorsed or rejected, and it is always derived by the [fold](/terms/fold.md) from [anam-events](/terms/anam-event.md). The artifact stores only its birth status, the state it was born in (`admitted` or `proposed`), which never changes. One truth per fact, no place overwritten.
