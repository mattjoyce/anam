Health data pipeline

How a heartbeat on the watch, a step on the scale, a night on the CPAP and a position from the phone become facets in Anam, which machine does each part, and how to tell when a part has stopped.

State as of 13 October 2026. The health ETL and healthdata.db that this page used to draw retired on 11 October (card 55); they appear below only as history.

where the data starts a collector and the file it writes an importer, a ductile plugin Anam

The shape

Every vendor source reaches Anam the same way (docs/IMPORTERS.md). A collector keeps the source's own data in its own format on the Projects share. A ductile plugin on the unraid gateway, from the anam-importers repository, reads that format, shapes each row to a Facet Definition and posts envelopes to the Anam daemon in batches of 49, under a token that is good for its source and its types alone. Anam knows no vendor: the gate checks each envelope against the Definition, admits what is new, supersedes what changed and answers no-op for what it already holds, so every importer can resend everything and nothing doubles.

Anam's own two formats arrive the same way. anam_records reads the records folder and anam_documents the documents folder with its sidecars; for each document, anam_submit posts the bytes to POST /v1/documents before its reference, and Anam keeps them in its library. Since card 119 the daemon reads no source itself, and the envelope route is the only way in.

The stages

  1. Collect from Garmin The garmindb container on unraid runs its own cron, four times a day. It downloads from Garmin Connect into healthdata/garmin/DBs/ (garmin.db, garmin_summary.db, the activity databases) and keeps every FIT file. The garmin plugin in the gateway checks garmin.db's modification time every 30 minutes and raises health.new_data with the days that changed.
  2. Import from Garmin Pipeline anam-garmin-sync runs anam_garmin on that event (handle, the changed days) and weekly by cron (poll, everything, labelled a snapshot so Anam knows the resend finished). It shapes nights to health.sleep-episode, daily heart rate to health.heart-rate-day, steps to activity.steps, the rest of the day to activity.day.garmin, activities to activity.exercise-session.garmin, and reads the FIT files for health.heart-rate-alert. Live since 11 October (card 55).
  3. Withings The withings plugin polls the Withings cloud every six hours into withings.db and raises one event per new measurement. anam_withings shapes each to health.measurement.withings and posts it; a weigh-in reaches the ledger in seconds. Live since 10 October (card 54).
  4. Blood pressure The phone app's CSV export is dropped into a watched folder on the share. bp_export_watch sees the new file and anam_bp_csv shapes every reading to health.blood-pressure. Exports are never moved, so a poll resends them all (card 80).
  5. DreamStation The CPAP's SD card is copied to healthdata/dreamstation/P-SERIES every few months. dreamstation_card_watch watches its LAST.TXT every 15 minutes, and anam_dreamstation decodes the card and resends every session as health.cpap-session.dreamstation (card 70).
  6. OwnTracks The phone posts its positions to webhooks.ductile.run/owntracks, a Cloudflare worker that spools them. The gateway pulls the spool and anam_owntracks posts an hour of positions as one place.track facet (card 130).
  7. Records and documents Hand-written YAML records (labs, medicines, travel, notes) and the document library live under anam/private on the share. The daemon reads them when the gateway's anam_ingest asks, hourly. An unchanged row is a no-op, so the hourly run costs nothing when there is no news.
  8. Anam The anam container on unraid holds the record at /mnt/disk2/appdata/anam/data: the ledger, the operational store, checkpoints and receipts, the library. It mints after every ingest that moves W, backs the record up nightly at 02:30 into /mnt/disk1/backup/anam/daily (14 kept), and serves the API, the inspector and the MCP adapter. Nothing else opens its files (.claude/skills/anam-service/SKILL.md).

What does not

  • Garmin minute samples. Millions of minute-level rows for heart rate, stress, respiration and blood oxygen. Daily figures were chosen instead; heart rate by the minute is card 129.
  • Weekly and monthly summaries. The ETL's weekly, monthly and yearly rows were retired with it. A weekly average is a computation over the record, a projection or a derived facet, not something that happened.

How to tell a stage has stopped

Every stage can stop without any error. The process stays up and the gateway stays healthy. The only reliable sign is that its output stops moving. Check the newest date each stage holds.

The ThinkPad gateway does this once a day, as job health_staleness, by running scripts/pipeline-staleness.sh from the Anam repository. It compares each stage with the one before it, and posts to Discord when one is more than two days behind.

ANAM_SERVER=http://192.168.20.4:8787 ~/Projects/anam/scripts/pipeline-staleness.sh

The queries it runs (as of 9b35e37; the script is the reference, not this page):

# 1. Garmin collected: newest day in GarminDB
sqlite3 "file:/mnt/Projects/healthdata/garmin/DBs/garmin.db?mode=ro" \
  "SELECT MAX(day) FROM daily_summary"

# 2. Withings collected: newest measurement
sqlite3 "file:/mnt/Projects/healthdata/withings/withings.db?mode=ro" \
  "SELECT MAX(date) FROM measurements"

# 3. Anam admitted Garmin days: the newest activity.day.garmin fact time, from the daemon
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $(cat ~/.config/anam/token)" \
  "http://192.168.20.4:8787/v1/timeline?zone=UTC&type=activity.day.garmin&from=$(date -d '-90 days' +%F)" \
  | jq -r '.entries[-1].fact_time'

# 4. Anam ingested Withings: the same for health.measurement.withings

A healthy pipeline has Garmin collected within two days of today, Anam's Garmin days within two days of GarminDB, and Anam's Withings rows within two days of Withings. Withings is not compared with today: a scale is stepped on when it is. Each importer's run history is in the unraid gateway:

/mnt/Projects/unraid_admin/unraid_cmd.sh \
  'docker exec ductile /app/ductile job logs --plugin anam_garmin --limit 5 \
   --config /app/config/config.yaml'

And what each source last sent Anam is in the daemon's status, since its last restart:

ANAM_SERVER=http://192.168.20.4:8787 ANAM_TOKEN_FILE=~/.config/anam/token anam status

How the record is kept

An envelope batch is admitted whole or refused whole. A resend-everything run (poll) labels its batches as parts of one snapshot, and GET /v1/snapshots/{id} says when it is complete and what the source no longer holds; nothing is retracted by an ingest, only reported. Retraction is an operator's command (anam retract, anam retire). After every ingest that moves W the daemon mints, so a read composes from checkpoints and replays only the tail. The nightly backup takes both databases in one write and copies the artifacts; a failure reaches Discord.

History: the ETL, and why it had stopped

Until 11 October 2026 Garmin and Withings reached Anam through a health ETL (ductile-healthdata, a one-shot container on unraid) that summarised both into healthdata.db by day, week, month and year, which a built-in health-db worker in Anam read hourly from the ThinkPad. Card 55 replaced it with the Garmin importer: 5,818 summary facets were retired, the ETL pipelines removed, and healthdata.db left as an archive nobody reads. The Anam worker was deleted on 13 October (card 50).

On 28 September 2026 Anam's Garmin data ended at 16 July. The ETL had been moved twice and neither move was finished.

Nothing alerted at any point. A pipeline that nothing triggers leaves an empty job log, and an empty log reads the same as a quiet day. That is why health_staleness compares outputs rather than watching jobs.
