# Genesis — from "my own Anam" to a committed spec

The opening session. It began as a mull — *"establish my own Anam, and we might expand it to include
all sorts of facets"* — and ended with a committed repo, a full spec doc set, and a clear build order.

## The arc

1. **Found the ground.** `/mnt/Projects/anam` was aspirational (empty); the real material was
   **Arch** (`/mnt/Projects/Arch/docs`) — Matt's person-centric
   clinical context doctrine — plus real health data already unified in `healthdata.db` (Garmin +
   Withings) and, added mid-session, a blood-pressure CSV sent over LocalSend.

2. **Named the core move.** Anam is Arch's machinery with **[[the-inversion|one decision inverted]]**:
   Arch refuses to be a longitudinal record (every read is episode-scoped by default); a personal soul
   *wants* to be exactly that. So the whole-person read becomes the default. From this followed
   [[person-substrate-grain]] and [[mint-unit-whole-timeline]].

3. **Absorbed the standards.** Matt pointed at his Go coding standards and the Ductile naming standard;
   those became `AGENTS.md` and drove choices like [[facet-types-dotted-namespaced]].

4. **Adopted a design lens.** Installed the `luminaries` skill and took
   **[[guiding-lens-hickey-armstrong|Hickey × Armstrong]]** as the standing lens. Running its
   *Before — design review* pass over the draft sharpened the spec: named the error kernel, quarantined
   the single-table braid, killed a second source of truth on the artifact, demoted the sync cursor to
   a cache.

5. **Found the endpoint and the reference.** The live DynamoDB Local sits on unraid at
   `http://192.168.20.4:8000` (static fake creds — Local doesn't authenticate). And
   [[reference-implementation-mint-cli|`mint-cli`]] is a working Go precursor with the exact
   store/ingest/fold shape — a big de-risk. It also settled [[facet-definition-format]].

6. **Wrote the spec into the repo.** `git init`, then the doc set mirroring Arch's:
   `docs/{ARCHITECTURE,GLOSSARY,MVP,PRFAQ}.md` + `AGENTS.md`, each committed. Decision to
   [[verbatim-payload-metadata-separation|hold payloads verbatim]] is load-bearing throughout.

## Where we are

Spec complete and committed on `master`. Nothing built yet in Go beyond the skeleton (module, Makefile,
ldflags versioning, check gate). Next: build-order step 1 — `facet` domain types, `config`, `clock`,
with table-driven tests — then the `store.DDB` layer against the live DynamoDB Local.

## Open threads

- Whether `identity_key` lives in `meta` or is derived from `source_ref` (see [[facet-definition-format]]).
- Retention / storage growth — deferred, tractable because Anam owns no facts.
- The eventual "all sorts of facets" expansion (`calendar.*`, `mail.*`, `note.*`) — the model already
