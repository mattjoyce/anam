# A domain head is added by decision, not earned by a type

**Decision (cards 192 and 222, 2026-10-21 and 2026-10-22).** A facet type is
`domain.concept[.specialisation]`. The domains are a closed set, nine since 22 October:
`health`, `activity`, `place`, `record` and `mind` live, `social`, `money`, `media` and `work`
reserved. Growth is in the concept, in a specialisation after it, and in the family, never in a
new head. A head is added only by a logged decision, as `mind` was for self-reported inner
states, with attention as its first concept.

## The tension

A new kind of fact arrives and nothing fits. The easy move is a new head named for the thing,
and the brands took that road once: `garmin.day`, `withings` as a head. Matt's objection was
that a brand at the top makes the concept unaskable; "exercise" was scattered under vendors.
The strict move is to force the fact under an existing head whether or not it belongs, which
makes the head a lie.

The decision is the middle: the set is closed, so a new head is rare and deliberate, but it is
not forbidden. Attention is not health and not activity; it is what the person says is going
on inside, and nothing in the eight heads meant that. A ninth head named for the domain, not
the fact, lets mood or energy join later without a tenth.

## Where a distinction is encoded

The companion rule, written on the explainer's page of that name, says which layer carries
which difference. A vendor's own shape takes the vendor leaf (`activity.exercise-session.garmin`,
`activity.day.garmin`), so the concept stays askable by prefix. A kind of a concept takes the
third segment (`health.medication.started`, `place.trip.flight`). A difference inside one shape
is a field, as `kind` is on `health.intake`. The head is the one place a type cannot invent.

## Why by decision

A head is a promise to every reader and every projection that names a prefix. Letting a type
earn one by existing would let an importer reshape the record's top level in a single
registration. The decision, with its log line, is the cost that keeps the set a set.
