## Recommendation

There is no project type. `topic` (card 222's payload field, one agreed spelling) is the join
key, and a project is a reading of everything with that topic: Matt's readings and declared
episodes, his commits, his desk photos. The join from a repo or a photo folder to a topic is data
in the query program, saved as a selection when it settles. Two of Matt's three questions are
answered by card 222 plus a query, with no inference. The inference is the last slice, a derived
facet of its own type under its own principal.

Sequence:

1. Card 222 lands (in progress): the three `mind.attention` types, the `attention` projection,
   Workbench and MCP entry. Answers "how long did deskcam have me" and "what absorbed me in June"
   from what Matt said.
2. Photos: no code. A `links` instance in `content` mode over the desk-photo folder, `root_name`
   the topic; EXIF `DateTimeOriginal` is already the fact time. The scheduled deskcam still drops
   a file in that folder, never into `/v1/documents`.
3. Card 226: `work.commit`, the co-author trailers as an array field so a query can test them.
4. The evidence query, one saved selection per topic, two worked questions in `docs/QUERY.md`.
5. Cards 212 and 213 when they come: a duration builtin and `count` turn tuples into figures.
6. The inference, as a new card, after Matt decides where endorsement lives.

## 1. The fact, and who authors it

A "project" braided three things: which facts belong, when it ran, and whose account it is.
Split, each has a place: the `topic` on a reading, the `repo` on a commit, the `root` on a
photo; each fact's own fact time; the source on the envelope. Nothing is left for a project type
to hold, and a type with no shape of its own is refused by the where-a-distinction page.

The join is the gap: no fact says `mattjoyce/deskcam` is `deskcam`. The cheapest place is the
program, as facts in the query text (`topic_repo("deskcam", "mattjoyce/deskcam").`), receipted
with the answer. When it settles, a saved selection holds it under a code, which is as close to a
first-class project as Anam needs; §4 says a bookmark is a stored query. Refused: an edge per
photo and commit to the started facet (fine by hand for one photo, but a thousand commits is a
thousand edges saying what one program line says); a `repos` field on the started facet (braids
the declaration with the evidence); a topic registry type (a Definition for a list the record
already holds).

`deskcam` lives in the facts that carry it. The rule for its shape is the Definition's `handling`
block (lower case, hyphens); each spelling is Matt's, by using it, and the list of topics is a
query, `topics(T) :- attr(_, "/topic", T).` Pitfall: a misspelling is a second topic, so the
entry form should offer the topics already in the record.

The inferred stretch, when it comes, is a facet of a new type, `mind.attention.inferred`: topic,
from, to, a one-line reason, the counts it leaned on. Its shape differs (a period, not a moment),
and it must not share Matt's series: `attention.episode` is keyed by topic alone, so a
model-written `mind.attention.ended` would close Matt's own episode through the fold. The review
dropped card 148's month bucket: the mint's bucket is checkpointing arithmetic, and the
inference's period is whatever the evidence shows.

## 2. The inference path

Nothing today writes a derived facet. `Provenance.Derivation` (method, model, watermark) exists
in `internal/facet` and no ingress fills it; the envelope has no field for it; the fold derives
`endorsed` and `rejected` from anam-events nothing emits. The smallest path:

- A principal `attention-model` in the importers file, types `[mind.attention.inferred]` only, a
  ductile plugin as card 158 recommends, because Anam runs no model. The review changed this:
  anam-mcp's `save_fact` asserts as Matt under source `conversation`, so it cannot carry a
  finding without braiding accounts. anam-mcp can propose in words; the fact needs its own token.
- The plugin reads through `POST /v1/query`, not dry-run, under a token with `receipts: always`
  (card 216), so what it read is on file. The envelope gains one optional block, `derivation
  {method, model, watermark, receipt}`, the receipt hash added to `Derivation`. The gate
  requires it from a principal marked `derived: true` and refuses it from any other, so a
  proposal never lands as a captured fact, nor the reverse. Provenance stays off the payload.
- No new status. "Proposed" is `admitted` with a derivation set; the type separates it in every
  projection, and `statuses: [endorsed]` on an inferred-only projection serves what Matt agreed
  with. Endorsing is `POST /v1/facets/{id}/endorse` and `/reject`, operator or workbench role,
  emitting the anam-events the fold already reads, beside `/retract`.
- Identity `{source}:attention/{/topic}/{/from}`. A re-run that keeps the start supersedes its
  own proposal; one that moves it is a new fact, and the old stays until Matt rejects it. The
  review dropped "stale": the proposal's watermark and the record's `W` are both readable, and
  a stored mark would be a place.

Pitfall: cards 93, 158 and 159 chose `standing` as a payload field; §11 and the fold chose
anam-events. Two mechanisms for one datum; see §6.

"record.link"], statuses: ["admitted", "endorsed"], max_classification: "Sensitive", shape:

How long did deskcam have me, from what Matt declared: each start paired with the end that
follows it in the series with no start between.

```
opened(S, From) :- entry(S, "mind.attention.started", From, _, _, _, _), attr(S, "/topic", "deskcam").
ended(X, To)    :- entry(X, "mind.attention.ended", To, _, _, _, _), attr(X, "/topic", "deskcam").
between(S, X)   :- later(S, M), later(M, X), entry(M, "mind.attention.started", _, _, _, _, _).
held(From, To)  :- opened(S, From), ended(X, To), later(S, X), not between(S, X).
open(From)      :- opened(S, From), not ended_after(S).
ended_after(S)  :- ended(X, _), later(S, X).
```

## 4. Sequencing

222 first: the only source for "what absorbed me" with nothing to infer, and it fixes the
spelling every later slice joins on. Photos second: configuration, not code. 226 third: a new
importer and a new head (`work`), each a decision of its own. The evidence query fourth. The
inference last, once there are weeks of readings to check it against.

Leave out a model-scored share of attention per period: no source measures it, a share forces a
closed world (every other topic named for one to be a fraction), and a number carries an
authority "held you May to July" does not. Leave out a stored stale mark, the kanban, vault and
purchase sources, and a project type.

## 5. Doctrine lines

Anam never judges content. The inference is a proposal with provenance, a separate type under a
separate principal, served through a projection that says so; Anam's verdict is nothing, Matt's
is an anam-event. Self-report and inference sit side by side: the gate's types list keeps the
model off Matt's types, and the series key keeps it out of his episodes. Agents committing as
Matt is handled by the importer keeping the trailers verbatim (card 226) and by the reader's
program excluding or weighting them, so the rule is in the receipt. Never by the importer
dropping commits or Anam deciding whose work a commit was.
